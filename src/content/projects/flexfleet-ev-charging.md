## Overview

Research-assistant project at the Institute for Power Electronics and Electrical Drives (ISEA), part of the E.ON Energy Research Center, within the Flexfleet project.

## What I did

- Developed a rule-based control algorithm for EV charging, evaluated against real charging data.
- Built an automated, scalable data pipeline processing more than 1,000 EV charging datasets from Germany.
- Prototyped a control algorithm that reduces estimated charging costs by 18 % in simulation.
- Currently developing ML-driven pipelines (Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Statsmodels, Pyomo) to forecast grid prices and optimise charging cost dynamically.
- Working towards a research paper on a reinforcement-learning model trained on synthetic and historical data.
